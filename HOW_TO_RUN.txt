HSN-Labs will look empty if you double-click index.html.

ES modules cannot load from file://.

1. Open a terminal in this folder
2. Run:  python3 -m http.server 8080
   or:   ./start.sh
3. In the browser go to:  http://127.0.0.1:8080

Three.js and cannon-es are bundled in vendor/ so the 3D rooms
do not need the internet after that.
